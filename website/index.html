Easy 2-Minute Setup

Zero accounts. Zero background drain.

OmniScreen runs privately on your home Wi-Fi or personal hotspot and syncs only when you tap Sync.

1

Open OmniScreen on Windows

Launch OmniScreen on your PC. It tracks active foreground PC time locally in SQLite and opens your Apple-style desktop dashboard.

2

Install the Android App

Install the signed APK and grant Usage Access. On Android 13/14, if Android shows "Restricted setting", open Settings -> Apps -> OmniScreen -> tap the 3 dots -> Allow restricted settings.

3

Scan QR & Sync Bidirectionally

Click + Pair Mobile on your PC and scan the QR code with your phone camera. Tap Sync Reality anytime to see combined exposure on both your PC and your phone.

Honest capabilities and real limitations.

No marketing hype. Here is exactly what OmniScreen does well today and where operating system sandboxes draw hard lines.

Platform / Area Status What Works & Honest Limitations
Windows 10 / 11 Public Beta Tracks active foreground apps and idle time locally. Must be running while you use your PC (does not read PC history retroactively when closed). Unsigned beta builds may show Windows SmartScreen ("More info -> Run anyway").

macOS & Linux Planned The core Hub runs natively on macOS and Linux; native desktop foreground sensors are planned after Windows + Android Beta stabilizes.
Security & Privacy Architecture

Your usage data never leaves your room.

Built from day one as local-first software with zero external servers.

Zero Cloud & Zero Telemetry

No sign-ups, no analytics SDKs, no remote databases. Every timestamp is stored in a single local SQLite file on your PC. Android Cloud Auto Backup is explicitly disabled (allowBackup=false).

Host-Locked Controls

Administrative actions (renaming, deleting devices, Wi-Fi TV scans, shutdown) are locked strictly to localhost (127.0.0.1) with strict local CORS, preventing browser CSRF or LAN tampering.

Trusted Wi-Fi Advisory

Local Wi-Fi sync uses direct LAN HTTP for zero-config pairing without custom TLS certificates. Sync on your trusted home Wi-Fi or personal mobile hotspot rather than untrusted public cafe Wi-Fi.
